About Me

Crafting native Android experiences that feel effortless

My name is Maulik Dadhaniya, and I am a passionate Mobile Android and Kotlin Developer with over 8 years of experience in mobile technologies. I specialize in building high-performance, scalable, and user-centric mobile applications using Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Android SDK, Coroutines, Flows, and modern Android architectural patterns such as MVVM, MVI, and Clean Architecture.

Throughout my career, I have worked across diverse domains, including fintech, multi-tenant platforms, and large-scale consumer applications. My expertise spans end-to-end mobile development, API integration, secure data handling, performance optimization, and crafting seamless UI/UX experiences. I'm always excited to explore new tools, frameworks, and best practices that push the boundaries of mobile innovation.
